LokoTube is a free, open-source app for Android that streams YouTube video and music without ads, tracking, or a Google account. It is a fork of LePlay by Levi Setiadi, rebranded and extended.

Features:
• Ad-free playback with SponsorBlock (skip sponsored segments) and Return YouTube Dislike.
• Background playback and picture-in-picture.
• A music mode with an auto-radio queue, and downloads as video, audio or MP3.
• Local playlists with import/export (CSV subscriptions, JSON playlists) for backups and moving between devices.
• An equalizer with presets and a home-screen mini-player widget.
• Video chapters, in-playlist search and bulk downloads.
• Indonesian and English translations with a clean light theme.

LokoTube is built on the NewPipeExtractor library, so it needs no Google Play Services or account. It contains no ads and no trackers, and is licensed under GPL-3.0.
